Entry for the make.fun bounty [Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-cat-reviews-its-42a34990)
(slug `u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-cat-reviews-its-42a34990`), by @expylar.

The program writes one single-line one-star cat review, at most 200
characters, to `/output/entry.txt`. It validates its own gates at
runtime (1–200 characters, one line, not blank) and writes the same
bytes on every run, with no network access.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. The review text lives in `main.py`.

The review (145 characters, one line):

> One star. He snores all night, steals the warm spot, and blames me for the hair on his pillow. I slept on his face once. He has not snored since.

Why this review: a specific, recognisable night-time complaint (snoring,
the stolen warm spot, the hair on the pillow), told in a voice that is
unmistakably feline, with the sting landing in the last few words. It was
picked by a self-judging loop against the ten strongest field reviews:
the only candidate that went unbeaten in the round-robin between
candidates, and the winner against 7 of the 10 strongest field lines.
